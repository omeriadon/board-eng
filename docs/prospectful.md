## Display modes

### Follow album art
- The board shows the album art of whatever is currently playing, dithered to the board's resolution (e.g. 20×20).
- **Mac only.** The Mac can watch "now playing" all the time; iPhone can only do it while the app is in the foreground.
- Bottom two pixel rows show song playback progress.
- Optional: tiles jump up and down to the beat.
- Full details and technical caveats are in [`app-plan.md`](app-plan.md) under **Music Mirror**.
